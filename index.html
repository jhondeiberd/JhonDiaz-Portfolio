About me

I’m a software developer with more than three years of professional experience building and maintaining C# / .NET Framework applications, REST APIs and SQL Server databases. My work has covered monitoring the applications and APIs that keep systems exchanging data, resolving production incidents, and developing new APIs, features and tools that business teams use every day.

Before moving into development full time, I spent twelve years in ERP applications (Microsoft Dynamics). I led the team that supported the ERP and other enterprise systems, managed the database, the budget and the licences, and approved new developments, projects and migrations.

In my ERP roles, I worked directly with area managers and end users. I listened to their needs, turned them into requirements, documented the solutions and supported the teams that depended on them. I bring the same approach to every project: understand the problem first, then build the solution that fits it.

I like to adopt new technologies early. I use AI assistants to speed up development and optimize code, and I always review, test and understand what I deliver.
